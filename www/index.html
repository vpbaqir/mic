Important compatibility note

This web app processes microphone audio in your browser. Android and browser versions control which output device receives the sound; a web page cannot guarantee Bluetooth routing on every phone. Bluetooth may also add noticeable delay. Use headphones or a wired PA input for lower latency when needed.

Troubleshooting

I can't hear my voice

Confirm the Bluetooth speaker is connected and selected as Android's media output. Raise Monitor volume gradually, then restart the microphone. Some browser/device combinations do not route real-time Web Audio to Bluetooth correctly.

The sound squeals or echoes

Lower the speaker volume immediately and move the phone farther from the speaker. The speaker output can re-enter the phone microphone and cause feedback.

Microphone permission failed

Allow microphone access in Chrome's site settings. Microphone access requires HTTPS or localhost. Close other apps that may be using the microphone.
