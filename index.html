If it doesn't work

I can't hear anything

Confirm the speaker is connected and selected as your phone's media output. Check the phone's media volume and MicLink's output volume. Some phone/browser combinations do not route live browser audio to Bluetooth reliably.

I can't allow the microphone

Open this page in Chrome or another modern browser over HTTPS. Check the browser's site permissions and close other apps that may be using the microphone.

There is a delay

Bluetooth audio can have noticeable latency. This depends on your speaker, phone and Bluetooth codec. A wired audio connection generally has less delay.

MicLink processes audio on your device. It does not upload or record your voice.
